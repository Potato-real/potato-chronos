About Bloom Valley Nursery

Our Story

Bloom Valley Nursery is a family-owned plant nursery dedicated to helping local gardeners and homeowners create thriving outdoor (and indoor) spaces. We specialize in high-quality plants, trees, shrubs, and gardening supplies while promoting sustainable growing practices.

Our goal is simple: make gardening accessible, enjoyable, and successful for everyone—from first-time plant parents to experienced green thumbs.

Hours of Operation

  • Monday – Friday: 9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.
  • Saturday – Sunday: 10:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.

We are closed on major holidays. Call ahead during severe weather.
